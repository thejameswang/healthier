Healthier Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

In short: Healthier does not collect, upload, sell or share your data. Everything the app reads from Apple Health is processed on your iPhone and stays there unless you choose to export and share it yourself.

What the app reads

With your permission, Healthier reads data from Apple Health (HealthKit) — for example workouts, heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep, steps, active energy, Activity rings and body measurements. You choose which types to allow in the Apple Health permission screen and can change this at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Healthier. Healthier never writes to Apple Health.

How it's used

Your data is used only to show you information inside the app and its widgets: readiness, training load, sleep, trends, workouts, discoveries and similar features, and to create export files when you ask for one. It is never used for advertising or marketing, and never used to identify you.

Where it's stored

  • Calculations happen on your device. The app keeps a cache of its results on your iPhone so it opens quickly; this cache is excluded from iCloud backups.
  • Widgets read a small summary of today's numbers stored in a container shared only between Healthier and its widgets, on your device.
  • Health data is not stored in iCloud and is not sent to any server. Healthier has no servers and no accounts.

Exports

When you create an export, Healthier saves a file on your device. It leaves your device only if you share it — for example with the share sheet, AirDrop or Files — and then it goes only where you send it. Once shared, that copy is subject to the privacy practices of whoever you shared it with (for example an AI service you upload it to).

What we don't do

  • No analytics, tracking or advertising SDKs.
  • No third-party data sharing or sale.
  • No collection of contact details, location, identifiers or usage data.

Notifications

Optional reminders (such as the weekly Replay or export completion) are scheduled on your device and never include health values.

Children

Healthier is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

Not medical advice

Healthier provides estimates for general fitness and wellness. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
