Frequently asked questions

Some of my data is missing

Healthier shows only what Apple Health contains and what you've allowed it to read. Check Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Healthier and turn on the data types you want. Workouts from Strava, Runna and other apps appear once they're in Apple Health (you can see them in the Fitness app).

Readiness doesn't show

Readiness needs about two weeks of heart rate variability or resting heart rate data, which usually comes from an Apple Watch worn overnight.

My Activity rings don't match my watch

Make sure "Activity Summaries" is allowed in the Health permissions for Healthier. The app then shows the same values and goals as your watch.

The widgets aren't up to date

Widgets show the numbers from the last time Healthier refreshed. Open the app to update them; after midnight they ask you to open the app until today's data is in.

I see duplicate workouts

Healthier hides workouts recorded by two apps at the same time (for example Apple Watch and Strava). To remove a duplicate from Apple Health itself, open Health → Browse → Activity → Workouts → Show All Data and swipe left on it.

How do exports work?

More → Export My Data creates a ZIP of CSV, JSON and GPX files on your device. Share it wherever you like — for example to ChatGPT, Claude, Excel or Python. Large exports continue in the background and resume if interrupted.

Privacy

Healthier has no accounts and no servers, and doesn't collect any data. Read the privacy policy.

Healthier provides estimates for general fitness and wellness and is not medical advice.
